Agentic Finance Tracker

Dominican bank alerts in Gmail become a live finance dashboard in Google Sheets, with daily and monthly summaries — and investments tracked from broker emails, with live prices and real returns. AI-built; quality-assured by Joaquín Gañán.

All data in the video is synthetic. The parser output, Setup Wizard, emails, the Holdings sheet, the broker paste dialog, Unrecognized and the Start here panel are renders of the real code; the Dashboard scene recreates its layout, since Google Sheets can't be rendered outside Sheets.
